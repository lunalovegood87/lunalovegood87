## Assumptions I made (correct any and I'll revise)

1. You are **over 29**, so the *genç girişimci* income-tax exemption (needs <29) does **not** apply. If you're under 29 it adds a large 3-year exemption — tell me.
2. Your METU postdoc contract **permits outside paid consultancy**. Full-time academic posts in Türkiye (2547 sayılı Kanun) often restrict this or route it through *döner sermaye*. **This is the biggest thing to verify** before registering anything — see `07-...`.
3. "Institutional/consultancy contracts" are won mainly via **targeted direct outreach** to specific organizations plus your existing network — hence the cold-email templates.
4. English is professional-fluent, Turkish native; deliverables and assets are bilingual.
5. You'll operate **solo** (no employees) and invoice as a *şahıs şirketi / serbest meslek erbabı*.
6. Realistic average **~4 hrs/week**, but you can **batch** (e.g., block a week to deliver a workshop).
7. You're willing to **bring foreign-currency payment into Türkiye** (Wise/Payoneer → TR bank), which the export-service exemption requires.
8. Goal is **steady side income**, not building an agency or scaling.
9. Your usable network: **METU TEKPOL, EU project consortia (CORDIS familiarity), Turkish academic/policy circles, and the international methods community** (RSiena, ABM, economic complexity).
10. Start date **now (July 2026)**; the plan runs 6 weeks from a standing start.
11. Rates quoted in **EUR**; USD roughly parity for planning.
12. A **static professional page / ORCID / university profile** is acceptable and is *not* what you mean by social media. Correct me if not.
