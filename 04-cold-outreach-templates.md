# 04 — Outreach Email Templates (Top 3) · EN + TR

Direct outreach, not marketing. Each email is short, names a specific reason you're writing *to them*, and asks for one small thing (a 20-minute call), not a sale. Send from your institutional or a professional address. **Personalise the bracketed lines — the first sentence must prove you looked them up, or it reads as spam.**

**Ground rules that keep this out of "self-promotion" territory:**
- One recipient, one reason, researched. No mass blasts, no BCC lists.
- Lead with *their* problem, not your CV.
- One clear ask. One follow-up only (template at the end). Then stop.
- Aim for ~150 words. If they reply, move to a call.

**Who to send to (per service):**
- **Service 1 (econometrics):** heads of research at boutique economic/policy consultancies; PIs of grant projects with an empirical component; evaluation units at NGOs/development banks.
- **Service 2 (simulation/networks):** coordinators of funded projects mentioning ABM/simulation/network analysis; research-group leads; consultancies with a modelling deliverable.
- **Service 3 (EU data/complexity):** Horizon Europe / Interreg consortium coordinators; regional development agencies; think tanks publishing complexity or diversification work.
