# 07 — Invoicing from Türkiye: The Lightest-Touch Route

**Not tax or legal advice.** This is an orientation so you walk into an SMMM (mali müşavir) meeting knowing the terrain and the right questions. Rules and thresholds change yearly — confirm current figures before acting. Sources at the end.

---

## The one thing to check first (before anything else)

**Does your METU postdoc contract allow outside paid income?** Full-time academic staff in Türkiye fall under *2547 sayılı Kanun*, which restricts outside employment and often routes external consultancy through the university's *döner sermaye* system rather than a private company. Depending on your exact contract (kadro type, full-time vs part-time), private consultancy may be:
- allowed directly,
- allowed only via *döner sermaye* (university takes a cut, handles invoicing), or
- restricted / requiring permission.

**This determines everything below.** Ask your department/HR in writing. If outside income is restricted, the *döner sermaye* route may still let you do the same work billed through the university — worth asking about explicitly.

---

## The big advantage you have: the export-service exemption

When you provide services **to a client abroad**, the service is **used abroad**, and payment is **brought into Türkiye in foreign currency**, two things apply:

1. **Income-tax exemption on exported services** — for qualifying service lines (the law lists *veri analizi / data analysis*, software, engineering, design, consultancy-type services among others), a large share of the profit is exempt from income tax. Recent rules put this at **80% of the qualifying income** exempt (verify the current rate and exact qualifying list with your SMMM — it has changed and has conditions).
2. **VAT (KDV) exemption on exported services** — services consumed abroad are generally VAT-exempt (*hizmet ihracatı istisnası*), so you don't add 20% KDV to foreign invoices.

**Why this matters for you:** your core offerings (econometric *data analysis*, modelling, consultancy) plausibly fall inside the qualifying lines, and your whole plan is foreign clients paying in EUR/USD. That's the exact fact pattern the exemption rewards. Confirm your specific NACE/activity code qualifies.

**Conditions to keep clean:**
- Invoice a **foreign** person/entity.
- The service is **used/benefited from abroad**.
- Bring the payment **into Türkiye as foreign currency** (Wise/Payoneer → a TL/FX bank account). Keep the bank's *döviz alım belgesi (DAB)* / SWIFT records as proof.
- Keep contracts and invoices showing the foreign client and service.

## The likely structure: *şahıs şirketi* as *serbest meslek erbabı*

For recurring professional consultancy, the standard low-overhead vehicle is a **sole proprietorship (şahıs şirketi)** registered as a **self-employed professional (*serbest meslek erbabı*)**, which issues an **e-serbest meslek makbuzu / e-fatura**.

**Rough setup:**
- Register with the tax office (vergi dairesi); get an activity code; enroll in e-belge (e-fatura/e-SMM).
- **Bağ-Kur** (self-employed social security) enrollment — a real monthly cost; note that some earlier *genç girişimci* Bağ-Kur relief was repealed at end-2025, so don't count on it.
- An **SMMM handles bookkeeping** — budget roughly **₺2,000–4,000/month** (confirm locally).
- **Genç girişimci istisnası** (up to ₺330,000/yr exempt for 3 years) requires being **under 29 and a first-time taxpayer** — assume it doesn't apply to you unless you're under 29.

**Withholding note:** *serbest meslek* income from Turkish payers is normally subject to 20% *stopaj* at source — but your clients are **foreign**, so they don't withhold Turkish tax. You declare and settle via annual return, with the export exemption reducing the taxable base.

## Decision tree (take this to the SMMM)

```
Does your METU contract allow outside private consultancy income?
├─ NO / only via döner sermaye
│    → Route qualifying work through university döner sermaye, OR
│      keep income occasional and within legal limits.
│      (Ask the SMMM what "occasional/arızi" allows.)
│
└─ YES
     ├─ Testing the waters, income still uncertain
     │    → Start with expert networks & occasional invoices;
     │      register the şahıs şirketi once income is proven.
     │
     └─ Committed, expecting recurring foreign income
          → Register şahıs şirketi (serbest meslek erbabı) now.
             Confirm activity code qualifies for the export exemption.
             Set up e-SMM/e-fatura + Wise/Payoneer.
             SMMM files monthly/quarterly; annual return claims the exemption.
```

## Questions to put to the SMMM (bring this list)
1. Given my METU academic post, can I hold a *şahıs şirketi* / *serbest meslek* registration, or is there a conflict? (Confirm alongside HR.)
2. Which activity code covers econometric data analysis / modelling consultancy, and does it qualify for the **hizmet ihracatı kazanç istisnası**? What's the current exempt percentage and conditions?
3. Confirmation that **foreign-client services are VAT-exempt** and how to document it.
4. Monthly all-in cost: bookkeeping + Bağ-Kur + e-belge.
5. Exactly what **FX documentation** (DAB, SWIFT, contracts) I must keep to defend the exemption.
6. Estimated **effective tax rate** on, say, €15,000/yr of foreign consultancy income after the exemption.
7. Whether starting with **occasional (arızi) income** before registering is viable, and its limits.

## Practical receiving-money setup
- **Wise / Payoneer** business or personal account to receive EUR/USD, then move to a Turkish bank as FX (preserves the export-exemption trail).
- Keep **every invoice, contract, and inbound-payment record**; the exemption is only as safe as your paperwork.
