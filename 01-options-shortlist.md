# 01 — Options Shortlist

Ten realistic income options matched to your exact skill set (R / Python-Mesa / Stata / NetLogo / RSiena / Quarto; panel econometrics, ABM, network models, EU + admin data harmonization, economic complexity, Turkish agri-economics; bilingual writing/editing/teaching) and to your constraints: **international clients, hard currency, under 5 hrs/week, no social media.**

**Channel key:** ✅ = inside your selected channel (institutional/consultancy contracts, won by direct outreach + network). ⚠️ = adjacent channel you did *not* select, included because the fit is unusually strong — reconsider.

Rates are realistic mid-points for someone at your level working direct (not through low-cost marketplaces), grounded in 2025–26 market data (sources at end).

---

### 1. Applied econometrics & statistical modelling consulting ✅ — *TOP 3*
**What:** You take a client's dataset and research question and deliver specified, estimated, and defensible models — dynamic panels (system/difference GMM), GEE, Driscoll–Kraay SEs, robustness and diagnostics — plus a clean write-up and reproducible R/Stata code.
**Who pays & why:** Boutique economic consultancies, policy-research firms, applied teams at development banks/NGOs, and academic groups with grant money but no in-house econometrician. They have data and a deadline and cannot defend advanced panel methods to a referee or a client. You are cheaper and faster than hiring, and more credible than a generalist data scientist.
**Rate:** €90–140/hr; typical project €1,500–6,000; day rate €600–900.
**Fit:** High demand, fully asynchronous, deliverable-based — ideal for <5 hrs/week. Broadest buyer base of anything here.

### 2. Simulation & network-modelling specialist: RSiena + agent-based models ✅ — *TOP 3*
**What:** Subcontracted modelling work packages — stochastic actor-oriented models (SAOM/RSiena) for longitudinal network data, and agent-based models in NetLogo or Mesa: design, build, calibrate, document.
**Who pays & why:** Research consortia and consultancies that won a project promising simulation or network dynamics but have nobody who can actually build it. RSiena competence in particular is globally scarce — a few hundred people worldwide do it well. Scarcity is your pricing power.
**Rate:** €110–180/hr; project €3,000–12,000. The premium is real because substitutes barely exist.
**Fit:** Highest differentiation, lowest competition, hard currency. Delivery is bursty but plannable around your schedule.

### 3. EU-project data harmonization & economic-complexity analytics ✅ — *TOP 3*
**What:** A self-contained data work package for a grant consortium: harmonize CORDIS / Eurostat / TÜİK / Comtrade into an analysis-ready panel, and/or compute economic-complexity diagnostics (ECI/PCI, product space, relatedness, diversification paths) for a region or country.
**Who pays & why:** Horizon Europe / Interreg consortium coordinators, regional development agencies, and think tanks. They win the grant, then discover the data plumbing and complexity metrics are a specialist job. Budgets are already allocated in person-months, and paying an external specialist is normal and audit-friendly.
**Rate:** €500–700/day or €60–90/hr as a subcontractor; a WP over a few months is €5,000–25,000.
**Fit:** Directly leverages your network (METU, EU consortia) and your CORDIS/complexity experience. Recurring and invoice-clean in hard currency.

### 4. Grant proposal methodology & impact writing (Horizon Europe / ERC / bilateral) ✅
**What:** You write or rebuild the quantitative methodology, evaluation design, and impact-pathway sections of research proposals — especially where modelling, causal identification, or complexity indicators are the selling point.
**Who pays & why:** PIs and grant-writing consultancies. A strong, referee-proof methods section materially raises win probability on €1M+ calls, so a few thousand euros is trivially worth it. Highly asynchronous, short bursts.
**Rate:** €1,500–5,000 per proposal (or €90–130/hr). Success bonuses are common and worth negotiating.
**Fit:** Short, high-value, bilingual-friendly (also works for TÜBİTAK-international and Turkish PIs applying to EU calls).

### 5. Expert-network consultations (GLG / Guidepoint / AlphaSights / Dialectica) ⚠️ — *STRONGLY RECONSIDER*
**What:** Paid 30–60 minute phone consultations on your niche expertise: Turkish agricultural economics and policy, regional innovation systems, Türkiye's science/tech policy, economic complexity of the Turkish economy.
**Who pays & why:** Investors, management consultancies, and corporates doing due diligence pay expert networks to reach exactly one qualified person on a narrow question. Your country + domain combination is precisely what they can't find internally.
**Rate:** $300–500/hr (paid per minute; a 45-min call ≈ $225–375). Near-zero per-call setup, fully async.
**Fit:** The single best match for your constraints — under 5 hrs, hard currency, no self-promotion, paid within weeks. Only excluded because you didn't tick the channel. Registering with 3–4 networks costs ~1 hour total. **Recommendation: opt in.**

### 6. Statistical / methods peer review & manuscript strengthening for hire ✅/⚠️
**What:** Independent statistical review of manuscripts before submission — checking and rewriting the methods, redoing or validating the analysis, improving reproducibility — for authors, research groups, or editing firms.
**Who pays & why:** Non-native-English research teams and academic editing companies pay to raise acceptance odds at good journals; some journals pay statistical reviewers directly. Reliable stats reviewers are scarce.
**Rate:** €150–500 per manuscript; €60–150/hr. Modest but steady and trivially asynchronous.
**Fit:** Comes both institutionally (contract with an editing firm ✅) and via marketplaces (⚠️). Low rate, but near-zero friction and good for filling gaps.

### 7. Reproducibility & replication audits (R / Stata / Python) ✅
**What:** Independent reproduction of a study's results from its code and data, or building a reproducibility pipeline — increasingly required by economics/policy journals and funders.
**Who pays & why:** Journal data editors, funders, and research organizations with mandatory replication or open-science policies, and few qualified people to run them. You read all four of their languages fluently.
**Rate:** €400–800 per paper; €70–110/hr.
**Fit:** Clean, bounded, async, hard currency (mostly US/EU institutions). A credible niche that also feeds referrals into options 1–3.

### 8. Advanced-methods short courses delivered to institutions ✅
**What:** A 2–5 day course sold to a foreign university doctoral school, research institute, central bank, ministry, or methods summer school: ABM for policy, RSiena for social networks, panel econometrics, or economic complexity — delivered live online.
**Who pays & why:** Institutions buy a rare specialist instructor for their cohort. They have training budgets and a shortage of people who can teach RSiena or policy ABM credibly. You already teach this at graduate level.
**Rate:** €1,500–4,000/day institutional; a short course €4,000–12,000.
**Fit:** High per-hour value; delivery is concentrated (one intense week), so it averages under 5 hrs/week across the year. Repeatable to new cohorts with the same materials.

### 9. Economic-complexity & regional-innovation diagnostics for consultancies / dev banks ✅
**What:** A commissioned analytical product: complexity and diversification diagnostics for a region or sector — the analytics engine behind a strategy report someone else sells to a government.
**Who pays & why:** Development banks, regional development agencies, cluster organizations, and boutique strategy consultancies that sell diagnostics to public clients but lack the quantitative core. You supply ECI/PCI, product-space, and relatedness analysis as a white-label deliverable.
**Rate:** €80–140/hr; project €4,000–15,000.
**Fit:** Squarely your research identity; institutional; hard currency. Longer sales cycle, so treat as a build-over-time line.

### 10. One-off methods product sold through an existing marketplace ⚠️ — *OPTIONAL / SLOW*
**What:** A self-contained paid asset with no audience-building: a recorded course ("System GMM in R" or "Policy ABM in Mesa") on an existing marketplace (Udemy/Leanpub/Gumroad), or a licensed harmonized dataset / code toolkit.
**Who pays & why:** Graduate students and analysts worldwide, reached by the *marketplace's own* audience — not yours. No posting, no personal brand required.
**Rate:** €20–60 per sale; realistically €50–400/month and slow to build. Front-loaded effort.
**Fit:** Included because you asked about products, but it's the weakest match: low rate, slow, and it leans on a platform rather than your network. Park it unless you want a passive line later.

---

## How the ten cluster

- **Fastest euro, least setup:** 5 (expert networks), 6 (peer review), 1 (consulting off your network).
- **Highest rate / scarcity:** 2 (RSiena/ABM), 8 (training), 4 (grant methods).
- **Best network leverage:** 3 (EU WPs), 9 (complexity diagnostics), 1.
- **Lowest tax friction to start:** 5, 6 (small, occasional) → then formalize for 1–3.
- **Skip unless bored:** 10.
