## What you're actually signing up for

You register once. When a client (an investor, consultancy, or corporate) has a question in your area, the network emails you a short brief: topic, date, length, fee. You accept or decline. If you accept, you do a 30–60 minute call and get paid. That's the whole loop. No selling, no profile-building, no content.

## What rate to set

Set your hourly rate at intake at **US$400–450/hour** (they pay pro-rata by the minute, so a 45-minute call ≈ $300–340).
- Don't go below **$300** — a low rate signals low seniority and you can't easily raise it later.
- You can always *accept* a specific lower-priced request if it's interesting; but your listed rate anchors everything.

## Compliance — the rules that keep you safe (read once, internalize)

These networks are used by investors, so there are hard lines. As an academic you're low-risk if you keep to these:

**Always**
- Speak only to your **general expertise, public information, and your own published research**.
- Sign the pre-call attestation and answer screening questions honestly.
- Disclose any conflict: a board seat, ownership, or paid advisory role relevant to the topic.

**Never**
- Never share **material non-public information (MNPI)** — anything price-sensitive about a specific listed company that isn't public. If a client fishes for it, say "that's outside what I can discuss" and the network will back you.
- Never disclose **confidential data from METU projects, EU consortia, or any client** — including unpublished datasets or results you're bound to keep private.
- Never discuss a company you currently advise/work for without that company's explicit approval.
- Never give **investment advice** ("should I buy X?"). You give expertise; they make decisions.

If a request touches a topic where you'd have to cross one of these lines, **decline the call**. Declining is free and normal.

## Türkiye money & tax (start simple, formalize as volume grows)

- **Getting paid:** networks pay by bank transfer or PayPal/Wise. Use a **Wise or Payoneer** account to receive USD/EUR, then bring it into Türkiye as foreign currency (keep the records).
- **Tax, early stage:** a handful of calls may fall under occasional (*arızi*) income — confirm the current threshold with an SMMM. Once it's recurring, fold it into the **şahıs şirketi / serbest meslek** setup so the **export-service exemption (80% income tax + VAT exemption)** applies. Full detail in `07-turkiye-tax-setup.md`.
- **One line for the SMMM:** "I'll receive foreign-currency fees from overseas expert-network consultations — how do I handle this cleanly, and at what point should I register?"
