All prices in **EUR, net of Turkish taxes** (you invoice this amount; tax handling is in `07-turkiye-tax-setup.md`). These are realistic 2025–26 rates for direct work at your level — above marketplace floors, below big-consultancy list prices. Quote the **package** first; fall back to the hourly/day rate only when scope is genuinely open-ended.

**Three rules that matter more than the numbers:**
1. **Never quote before a 20-minute scoping call.** Price the problem, not the hours.
2. **Anchor high, discount deliberately.** Open near the top of the range; a first-client or referral discount is a favor you grant, not a rate you start at.
3. **Get 40–50% upfront** on fixed projects; balance on delivery. For new/unknown clients, a small paid pilot beats a big unpaid promise.

## Quoting cheat-sheet

- **Small/first client, want the reference:** bottom of range, and say so explicitly ("introductory rate for a first project") so the next quote isn't anchored low.
- **Well-funded consortium or corporate:** top of range or above; they read a low number as low quality.
- **"Can you do it cheaper?":** reduce *scope*, not rate. Drop a deliverable, don't discount the day.
- **Currency:** invoice in EUR (or USD at ~parity). State that FX conversion and any bank fees are the client's side.
- **Payment terms:** 40–50% on start, balance on delivery; net-15. New client → paid pilot first.
- **What you don't do for free:** scoping call yes (20 min); a written proposal, a test analysis, or a "quick look at our data" — no. That's the work.
